**Introduction**
Problem Statement and Requirements
Our group decided to do the Insurance Claim Management System Project, which
required us to develop an application for clients to apply claims that are then sent to members of
an insurance company, like a claim and finance manager, to evaluate their claim accordingly. The
claim management system provides a clear and easy to use GUI interface for all users involved
(Client, Claim Manager, Finance Manager, and Admin). As mentioned earlier, the client is able
to apply and then view the status of their claims, and also send messages to anyone who they
deem necessary. The Claim Manager evaluates claims that the clients apply and determine
whether or not they are eligible for compensation. From there, the Claim Manager can send
claims over to the Finance Manager who is in charge of giving the clients proper compensation
for their accidents if they are eligible for it. The Admins for this management system are in
charge of keeping the system clean and up to date. They are in charge of approving and denying
new accounts, managing different users’ accounts in the system and overall just making sure that
the application runs smoothly. All users in the system also have the ability to edit their accounts
and send messages to whomever that has a valid account.
